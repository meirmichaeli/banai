Findings & recommendations

Medium

M1 · The whole page scrolls, so the top bar can move out of view

The document is 541 px taller than the window. Scrolling the Properties panel or bringing an element into view moves the entire page, and the top bar (Save, Render, Show interior) goes off screen.

Recommendation: lock the app to the viewport (html, body { height: 100% ; overflow: hidden }, a grid with min-height: 0 on the workspace) so only the panels scroll. Upstream PR #50 also mentions a page-scroll fix, so check it first to avoid a conflict.

Medium

M2 · A tooltip can stay on screen after its element changes

After selecting a compartment, the panel re-rendered and moved, but the tooltip (“בחר את התא כדי לערוך אותו”) stayed where it was even though the mouse was no longer over its element.

Recommendation: in ui/tooltip.js, hide the tooltip when its target is removed from the DOM or moves (check target.isConnected on each store or language change, or use a MutationObserver), and when the panel scrolls.

Medium

M3 · The selected part's editor opens out of view

When you pick a part from the compartment list or כל החלקים (lower in the panel), its edit section (נבחר · …) is inserted at the top of the panel, and you have to scroll up to find it.

Recommendation: after a selection, scroll the Properties panel to the selected section (scrollIntoView({ block: 'nearest' })), or show the edit section next to the list item that was clicked.

Low

L1 · Field errors are squeezed into the narrow 3-column row

The height error wraps onto 3 lines under a narrow input, which is hard to read.

Recommendation: show errors for the Width/Height/Depth row once, at full width below the row, or use a shorter message (“מספר בס״מ בלבד”).

Low

L2 · The selected interior part's label shows through closed doors

With a shelf selected and “Show interior” turned off, its orange label floats in front of the closed door.

Recommendation: hide the label while the doors cover the part, or keep the interior open while an interior part is selected.

Low

L3 · A new drawer is hard to spot

“+ Drawer” adds a white drawer front at the bottom of a white compartment, with nothing to show what was added.

Recommendation: select the new item automatically (highlight plus label), and draw a visible outline and handle on drawer fronts.

Low

L4 · Dark handles nearly disappear on dark doors

On navy (and black or anthracite) doors, the black handles have very low contrast.

Recommendation: pick the handle colour from the door colour's lightness (light handles on dark doors).

Note

N1 · Saved designs are lost when the local backend restarts

Locally, the backend uses its in-memory store, because SUPABASE_SECRET_KEY is empty in backend/.env. Links work until the server restarts.

Recommendation: add the banai-dev secret key (Supabase dashboard → API keys) to save to the real database.

Note

N2 · The chat history is not restored with a saved design

Reopening a link restores the design but not the conversation. This is consistent with the spec (designs are what get saved), but users may expect it.

Recommendation: keep as is, or show a short note in the chat, e.g. “נפתח עיצוב שמור” (a saved design was opened).

Note

N3 · Writing in Hebrew switches the whole UI to Hebrew

This is by design: one language setting for the whole app, and the chat follows the language of the message. A user who works in English but types one Hebrew word will see the whole interface flip.

Recommendation: consider auto-switching only the chat reply language, and leave the interface language to the switch.
